About

I work on theoretical cosmology and gravitation, focusing on dark energy, modified and massive gravity, and the cosmological tensions in current observations. I teach physics at Sharif University of Technology and supervise students in research.

Research

Dark Energy & Cosmological Tensions

Phase transitions and Ginzburg–Landau-type models of the dark sector, aimed at easing the Hubble and other observational tensions.

Cosmology · Dark energy

Massive & Modified Gravity

Gravitation · Perturbations
